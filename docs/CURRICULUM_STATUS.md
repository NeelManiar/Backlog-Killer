## Verification procedure

Fetch the official landing page and linked subject syllabus PDFs, preserving TLS verification. Check the document's published academic/exam year, authority, grade and subject code against the selected profile, and record exact URLs, retrieval/verification date and preferably artifact hashes in a reviewed catalog revision. Follow only authoritative subject publications. Validate every official unit ID/title against the document, including deletions and Class 11/12 separation. Never infer missing units from the old demo or generic lists.

`src/data/curriculum.ts` defines a versioned catalog contract: ID, kind (school/entrance), authority, grade, exact year, subject/code, official source URL, status, verified date and unit IDs/titles. Promote a catalog to verified only after human/source review. Add regression fixtures asserting the exact official list and exclusions. All currently pending entries have empty units and null verified dates/codes.

Progress is keyed by an explicit canonical `progressKey`; overlapping units may share that key only after reviewing actual content equivalence. Shared keys collect school/exam catalog IDs and preserve one progress record. Similar titles alone never imply equivalence. Grade, board, subject selection, academic year and examination year are filtered independently. Changing a profile updates catalog links and adds missing canonical chapters without deleting old progress.

Custom chapters remain clearly labeled and are scoped to School or the selected exam section. They are not a verified syllabus. The app continues to support its offline v0.2 tools while verified content is unavailable.
